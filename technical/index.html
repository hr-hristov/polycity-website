TECHNICAL

Three ways
to run a city.

A quick estimate for the whole city, a simulated day for every resident, and a junction study for one corner, second by second.

Follow the three ↓

01 · QUICK ESTIMATE

Four steps,
the whole city.

The city is cut into zones. The estimate counts the trips each zone makes, spreads them between zones, shares them between the ways of travelling and loads the car trips onto the roads, hour by hour. It works in totals, so nobody leaves earlier or stays at home.

Trips madeFrom the census
Where they goFitted to the travel survey
How they travelCar, public transport, bike, on foot
Which roadsCar trips, hour by hour

02 · SIMULATED DAY

Every resident,
a day of their own.

The main way Polycity runs a city. Every resident is modelled from the census and the travel survey: a home, an income, a car or not, and a routine for the day. Everyone travels at once, so tailbacks reach back into side streets. Each day is weighed, some residents try another route, hour or way, and round after round the city settles.

03 · JUNCTION STUDY

One corner,
second by second.

The study takes the vehicles and routes the simulated day settled on, and replays one corner second by second. Every car, bus, tram, lorry and bicycle keeps its lane, its gap and its turn.
