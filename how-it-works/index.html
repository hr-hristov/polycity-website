From a question
to a decision.

Bring your city’s data and match today. Then draw a change, simulate a day and compare what happened.

01 · BRING THE CITY

Start from your
city’s own data.

Your organisation studies one city. Its street map and public transport timetable come with it. You add the census, the zones, a travel survey and traffic counts.

THE DATA · OPEN STANDARDS

Built from
open standards.

The street map and the timetable are read as OpenStreetMap and GTFS files, and the zones as GeoJSON. No open standard exists for a census, a travel survey or traffic counts, so those arrive as plain tables. Every other feed Polycity reads has its own open standard.

02 · MATCH TODAY

Match today
before changing it.

Polycity builds today’s city and fits it to your travel survey and traffic counts. Your team checks it and approves it. Every scenario starts from that approved city.

03 · DRAW THE CHANGE

Draw the change
on the map.

Close a street, change a junction, run a tram more often, price the parking or build new homes. Choose where each change applies and when: some hours, some dates, or from an opening year.
