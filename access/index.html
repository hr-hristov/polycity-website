Polycity opens one city at a time, with the people who will use it. Write to us with the change you are weighing, and we will say what Polycity can answer for it and what it cannot.

↓
After you write

No form.
A conversation.

One address reaches the people who build Polycity. There is no sign-up, no trial to start on your own, and nothing on this website to sign in to.

01

We answer honestly.

You describe the street, the line or the fare you are thinking about changing. We answer with what Polycity would work out for it, and where it would leave you guessing.

02

We build your city.

Your street map, your timetable and what people do today become a model of a whole day in your city. You see how far it sits from the traffic counts you already hold, before you trust it.

03

Your team signs in.

Everyone works from one approved city. Owners, planners, analysts and readers each get what their work needs, and nothing beyond it.

What a city brings

Three things,
and most cities
already hold them.

Polycity starts from what a transport department keeps anyway. Nothing about a named person is wanted, and nothing about one is used.

01

A street map.

Streets with their speed limits and how many lanes they carry. An open street map is enough to begin. Your own road base makes the answer sharper.

02

A timetable.

Bus, tram and metro lines with their stops and their times, in the published timetable files most cities already hand to journey planners.

03

What people do today.

A travel survey, counts from your counting stations, or both. They decide how close the modelled day comes to the day you can measure.

Polycity has no sign-in on this website, and this website has no way into the product. Access is opened by us, for a named city and a named team.
